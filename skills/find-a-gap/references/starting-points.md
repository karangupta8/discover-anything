# Starting Points

Gaps identified in October 2026, for one person with a coding agent. Re-check
each before recommending it: these age quickly. Effort: S (days), M (weeks),
L (months).

## Astronomy

| Gap | Known answers | Confirms | Effort | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Score preregistered predictions when their test data arrives (e.g. TIC 4206066 in TESS Sector 110, observed 31 Oct to 26 Nov 2026) | The predictions themselves | The predictor; public write-up | S | One of the first independent checks of a preregistered amateur prediction |
| Independent review of published amateur candidates | Recovered published planets | Their authors; ExoFOP | S–M | Several authors publicly asked for independent review |
| Variable stars in recent TESS full-frame images (Sectors 83+, bright stars) | VSX variables and TESS EB catalogue members in the field | AAVSO VSX moderators | M | No telescope time needed. The TESS Ten Thousand Catalog covers EBs in Sectors 1–82 only. Others are active here: check VSX and recent repositories first |
| Asteroid rotation periods from TESS full-frame images | Asteroids with periods in the Lightcurve Database | Minor Planet Bulletin | M | Most asteroids lack measured periods |
| Archival transit timing of known planets (NASA Exoplanet Watch, EXOTIC) | Published ephemerides | AAVSO exoplanet database | S | Most certain contribution, least novelty |
| Single transits and "duotransits" pipelines ignore | Known long-period planets with single TESS transits | TESS follow-up community | L | Much less crowded than short periods; ambiguous periods |
| Active asteroids in DECam archives | Known active asteroids | Active Asteroids team; MPC | L | Automates a Zooniverse project's visual search |
| Co-moving brown dwarfs (Gaia DR3 + WISE proper motions) | Known wide companions | Backyard Worlds team | L | |
| Rare-transient filters on public alert brokers (Fink, ANTARES, Lasair) | Archived classified transients | Broker teams; TNS | L | Time-critical |

**Usually not worth it:** discovering new asteroids in archives (credit goes to
surveys; about 27,000 already found this way in one 2024 effort); another
short-period TESS sweep of samples already searched in October 2026.

## Historical archives

| Gap | Known answers | Confirms | Effort | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The unknown eruption of 1808/09 | Every other eruption the pipeline re-finds | Volcanologists | L | Newspapers searched without success; best lead is about 1,235 untranscribed Dutch East India Company logbooks (needs handwriting recognition) |

## Other fields with the same shape

Public data, known answers, a reachable specialist. Use the core loop and record
traps in `LESSONS.md`:

- Herbarium and museum specimen records (range changes, mislabelled specimens)
- Historical weather logs (ship logbooks against reanalysis)
- Old scientific literature (forgotten measurements, unrecorded observations)
- Open government data (registers that should agree but don't)
