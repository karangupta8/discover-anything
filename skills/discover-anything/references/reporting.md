# Reporting

Where results go and how to share them without overstating them. The specific
official channels for each domain are in the domain packs.

## Order of operations

1. Public repository and write-up (always): code, funnel, claim cards including
   rejections, reviews and responses
2. Preregistration with a DOI (Zenodo) before new data, where it applies
3. Human expert review: forum, direct email, the relevant citizen-science team
4. The official channel in the domain pack, only after 1 to 3
5. Optional: a short research note once there is a result worth citing

Zenodo gives a DOI for data, code and predictions. CC BY 4.0 for results and MIT
for code is a common pattern.

## Sharing publicly

Public posts bring reviewers, collaborators and corrections. Post only **after**
the repository (and preregistration DOI, if any) exist, so every reply can be
answered with evidence.

### Where people looked in 2026

| Where | Good for |
|---|---|
| Reddit r/ClaudeAI and similar agent communities | The method and the tooling |
| Hacker News "Show HN" | Technical stress-testing. Link the repository or write-up, and stay to answer questions with numbers and files |
| Bluesky | Reaching professional scientists. Lead with the ask: "could you sanity-check this?" |
| Domain forums (AAVSO, Cloudy Nights, r/askastronomy, history societies) | Expert critique before going official. Frame it as "what am I missing?" |
| A long-form post or blog | The full story the other posts link to |

One post per community; read each community's self-promotion rules first.

### A structure that explains well

1. What the instrument or archive is, in one sentence a child understands
2. What you saw, in plain words
3. Why it would matter
4. The check that makes it believable ("I hid each year and predicted it from the
   others: right 3 out of 3")
5. The numbers in human terms (Earth sizes, light-years, the date and town)
6. What you are less sure about
7. How it was done, with the agent and reviewers credited accurately
8. What happens next, and when (the preregistered test date)
9. Links: repository, DOI, write-up

### Non-negotiable when sharing

- "Candidate" appears in the post itself, not only in the repository
- No "NASA confirmed", no "AI discovered"; say what was actually approved or found
- Pin the caveats and the false-positive figure
- Credit whoever inspired the work and anyone who got there first
- Correct exaggerated reshares politely, publicly and quickly
- If the preregistered test fails, post that too, in the same place
