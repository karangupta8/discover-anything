## Lessons

1. **The bottleneck moved.** Writing a pipeline used to be the barrier. Now it is
   knowing which control to run, and having the discipline to believe the control
   over the excitement.
2. **The best amateurs out-rigoured the critics.** The sharpest criticism of
   one hunt came from that hunter's own null runs. Be your own harshest referee and
   the outside critique has nothing left to say.
3. **Preregistration is the amateur's superpower.** No credentials needed; it
   converts "trust me" into "watch this". A DOI dated before the data is worth more
   than any number of plots.
4. **Gaps close in days now.** Several people found the same signals within 72 hours.
   Check who else is searching before, during and after.
5. **Pick targets where the output doesn't burn other people's telescope time.**
   Variable star catalogs and asteroid periods are complete contributions on their
   own. Planet candidates need someone else's night to finish.
6. **Agents are best as tireless junior researchers, not oracles.** Let them write
   1,000 scripts; never let them be the only judge of what the scripts mean.
7. **Calibrate every test on known answers.** One hunter found an uncalibrated neighbour check
   would have rejected 31% of known planets. Defaults from a paper are not
   calibrated for your sample.
8. **Rejection lists are as valuable as candidate lists.** They show the funnel is
   honest and stop others from wasting time on the same impostors.
9. **Curiosity and rigour are not opposites.** The 3D fly-through and the funnel
   table belong in the same repo.
10. **The method transfers across fields.** The planet hunters and the archive
    researchers never coordinated, and converged on the same discipline: known
    answers first, a cheap-to-expensive funnel, checks against the primary record,
    the specialists' own catalogues, and honest status labels.
11. **A controlled negative is a result.** A pipeline that finds every known
    eruption and then finds nothing for 1808/09 says something about the sources.
    An uncalibrated one says nothing.
